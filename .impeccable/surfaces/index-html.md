## Scope
The whole app, index.html: editor (4 steps) and the read-only shared/personal views. Mode: Operate. Build path: code-led (Operate surface; no image generation).

## Audience and task
One friend settles a trip, night out, or shared flat on a phone, then shares to WhatsApp. Recipients open a link and need their own share at a glance.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Each friend owns a vivid color, and that color is the thread through the whole calculation: chip, donut slice, transfer row, breakdown. Refuses the grey form-wizard with one teal accent.
OWN-WORLD: White ground, cool hairlines (#E6E9F2), soft-offset shadows, 16px radii. Royal blue #2F5BFF for primary action and active step, green for add-expense and WhatsApp, a 10-color vivid person palette (pink, violet, blue, orange, teal...). Rubik 400-800, tabular numerals. Manual dark mode toggle, light by default.
STORY: Add people, add expenses with type icons, see the donut of who carries what, then who pays whom, then share.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Header (brand, example, theme). Hero: headline right, live status card left (avatar stack, total, counts). Four step tabs with icons below; active tab blue-tinted. Then the step's work card.
FORM: pinned by user reference image (middle panel); seed: none (pinned direction, no roll).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
